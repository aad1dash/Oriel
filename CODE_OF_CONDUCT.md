# Code of Conduct

Oriel should be a place where careful questions, honest uncertainty and generous technical disagreement are welcome.

## Our standard

When participating in the project:

- be respectful and specific;
- critique ideas and evidence, not people;
- make room for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- ask before making assumptions about another person's intent;
- acknowledge mistakes and repair harm when possible;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, sustained personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. This standard applies in the repository and in public spaces when someone is representing Oriel.

## Resolving problems

If a conversation becomes unproductive, pause and return to the concrete claim, evidence or decision. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently exclude someone when that is necessary to protect the community.

To report conduct privately, use a private maintainer contact listed by the project. If none is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact route without including sensitive details. Reports will be handled as discreetly and fairly as the circumstances allow. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
